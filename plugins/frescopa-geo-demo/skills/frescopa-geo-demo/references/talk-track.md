# Talk track — how the skill maps to the partner demo script

Load only when the presenter asks for narration help. The full script is the "Frescopa partner demo" doc; this is the cheat sheet for what the skill puts on screen at each moment.

## Where the skill fits (six sections, ~20–25 min)

| Script section | What the skill does | Line to land |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Web content management, then and now | Nothing (slides only) | "Your next customer may never see your homepage. Their AI assistant will read it first." |
| 2. Brand visibility from Coworker | Stage 1: seven signal artifacts | "Frescopa gets mentioned in AI answers but never cited. That gap is the action list, and we are going to act on item one right now." |
| 3. Governance | Stage 2 brand context + governance check, Stage 3 approval | "AI wrote this draft, but it did not get the final word." |
| 4. Page creation | Page menu, Stage 4 create + preview in `drafts/<name>/` | "This page exists because of item one on that action list." |
| 5. Human in the loop | Hand-off: presenter opens the edit URL | "Humans are still in control, and the agent is still right here." |
| 6. Publish | Nothing; a person publishes in Experience Workspace | "The next time Brand Visibility runs, it tells us whether it worked." |

## Five signal layers → what's on screen (Section 2)

| Layer | Artifact |
|---|---|
| AI prompts (what customers ask AI) | Topic-coverage table: 14 high-interest topics, Frescopa absent from 11 |
| Market (who AI recommends instead) | Competitor mentions vs citations chart: Nestlé/Keurig/Nespresso cited, Frescopa 0 |
| SEO (topics you rank for that AI overlooks) | Ranking-keywords table + leader scale (single-digit keywords vs ~190K for Nespresso) |
| Agentic traffic (can agents reach and read your pages) | Narrate: the page we build is on Edge Delivery Services, fast and structured for crawlers |
| Owned analytics (which pages drive revenue) | Narrate only; not pulled live (AA + CJA close the loop) |

The first two say what to fix, the next two why it's happening, owned data where to act and what it's worth. The reconciled Stage 1 headline IS the prioritized action list.

## Narration notes
- **Profee** has no ABV brand of its own; its pages use the Frescopa Coffee Pods signals.
- **Fallback:** if the skill renders from the snapshot, always say "live refresh pending".
- **Drafts:** remind presenters to end the prompt with "Save the page under drafts/<your-name>".
- **Real customers** (e.g. Tractor Supply): don't use this skill; its IDs are Frescopa-only. Use a plain prompt such as "Run an SEO and GEO gap analysis for <domain> against its top organic competitors." Semrush works for any domain; ABV only for onboarded companies.

## Watch-outs
- Don't say "most complete intelligence layer in the market" until claims approval says you can.
- Prompt-count figure on the five-layers slide (318M vs 289M): align on one before presenting.
- Confirm Experience Workspace positioning claims before using them with a customer.
- Frescopa analytics and outcome numbers are demo data.
